## Core Mechanics: How Idle Breakout Reinvents the Classic Brick Breaker

At its foundation, the game takes the timeless Atari Breakout loop and removes the paddle. Instead of scrambling to keep a single ball in play, your screen becomes an automated demolition zone populated by dozens of specialized bouncing spheres. Every brick on screen displays a numerical durability rating. Clicking a brick reduces its value by your manual click damage, while autonomous balls bounce around the playfield dealing damage on collision.

As bricks crack and vanish, they deposit cash into your bank. You reinvest these funds into buying more balls, boosting their speed, and enhancing their raw impact power. Unlike standard arcade titles, defeat is impossible; progression is purely a measurement of how efficiently your automated swarm shreds brick health pools that scale into the trillions.
