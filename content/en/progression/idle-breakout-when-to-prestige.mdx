## Understanding the Prestige System in Idle Breakout

Prestige mechanics serve as the engine of all incremental progress. In Idle Breakout, prestige resets your active board, your current stage level, your cash balance, and all basic ball purchases. In exchange, the game rewards you with prestige currency—often referred to by the community as Black Balls (BB) or prestige points—which fuel permanent, run-altering upgrades in the progression shop.

Many newcomers fall into the trap of clinging to their expensive ball collection. Seeing a high-tier ball firing across the screen creates an emotional attachment, but raw ball level without prestige multipliers cannot keep up with exponential brick health scaling. Community reports consistently highlight that holding onto an underperforming run out of fear of resetting is the number one reason players get stuck in early progression plateaus.

Before determining your exact reset point, you must understand what survives the wipe.

| Game Element | Status After Prestige | Notes & Strategic Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Current Stage Level** | Resets to Level 1 | You must replay stages, but increased speed makes this trivial. |
| **Active Cash Balance** | Resets to $0 | Spending leftover cash right before resetting provides zero value. |
| **Purchased Balls** | Completely Cleared | You rebuild your loadout using enhanced early-game multipliers. |
| **Prestige Upgrades** | Permanently Retained | All upgrades bought with prestige currency stay active forever. |
| **Achievements & Badges** | Permanently Retained | Passive bonuses unlocked via milestones never disappear. |
| **Earned Prestige Currency** | Credited to Bank | Added immediately upon confirming the reset dialog. |

Your prestige payout scales directly with the highest stage completed and the total number of bricks destroyed during that specific run. Because brick health balloons exponentially while linear ball upgrades suffer from sharp diminishing returns, pushing deep into stages without prestige multipliers becomes mathematically inefficient.

## Golden Rules to Maximize Every Prestige Cycle

To ensure you extract maximum value out of every single loop, follow these proven gameplay strategies gathered from experienced community playtests.

### 1. Never Bank Cash Before Resetting
Unlike prestige currency, normal gold and cash vanish completely when you prestige. However, you should check if spending that cash can push you past one more boss tier or achievement threshold. If buying three more upgrades does not immediately gain you another stage clear, do not waste time micromanaging upgrades—trigger the prestige immediately.

### 2. Time Your Runs Around Active vs. Idle Play
If you plan to leave the game running overnight or during a workday, do not reset right before stepping away. A fresh prestige run requires active input to purchase initial balls and establish board control. Instead, push an existing run as deep as possible overnight, then prestige when you sit down for an active play session.

### 3. Maintain the 10x Rule in the Late Game
In the deep late-game, seasoned players use the "10x Rule" to govern their prestige timing. As a rule of thumb, do not reset until the prestige points earned from the current run equal at least 5 to 10 times your total lifetime prestige investment. Resetting for anything less yields diminishing returns that slow down your long-term progression speed.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Idle Breakout Prestiges

### In idle breakout when to prestige for the very first time?
For your very first run, you should prestige between Level 200 and Level 250. Pushing earlier than Level 200 does not award enough Black Ball currency to purchase meaningful upgrades, while pushing past Level 250 without multipliers takes too long to justify the effort.

### Do I lose my achievements when I prestige?
No. All achievements, trophies, and permanent milestone unlocks remain active permanently across all prestige cycles. Their passive bonuses will continue to benefit your ball speed, damage, and clear rates on every subsequent run.

### Why does my stage progress feel slower right after prestiging?
Right after resetting, you return to Level 1 with no balls on the board. While your permanent prestige multipliers are active, you still need a few moments of active clicking or purchasing low-cost balls to kickstart your momentum. Within five to ten minutes, your enhanced multipliers will propel you far past your previous milestones.

### How do I know if I stayed in a run too long?
You have stayed too long if high-tier bricks linger on the screen for more than ten seconds or if clearing a single level takes multiple minutes. When your currency gain per minute flattens out, you have passed your optimal efficiency window and should prestige right away.
