### The Power of Status Effects: Poison and Plasma

In the early game, raw speed on Basic Balls easily clears boards. However, as brick values exceed millions, standard kinetic damage drops off. To unlock high-stage milestones without waiting days, Poison Balls become mandatory. 

Poison Balls apply a damage-over-time debuff that spreads across adjacent bricks or ticks down high-health targets independently of raw ball power. Pairing Poison Balls with Plasma Balls—which deliver splash damage across clustered blocks—creates a chain reaction that clears complex screens in seconds.

## Top Tips and Common Pitfalls When Grinding Achievements

Maximizing your achievement output requires balancing active gameplay habits with optimized offline progress. Avoiding common traps will save you hours of unnecessary waiting.

- **Do not over-invest in Basic Balls late:** While upgrading initial balls feels satisfying, Basic Balls offer poor returns beyond Level 100. Shift your capital toward Plasma and Poison variants as soon as they become affordable.
- **Prioritize Speed over Raw Power:** Fast balls strike more bricks per minute. In *Idle Breakout*, total collision count often triggers more useful interactions and cash gains than slow, single heavy hits.
- **Focus on High-Value Bricks:** Keep an eye out for golden or specially marked bricks. Prioritizing clicks on these targets yields massive temporary payouts that can finance major ball upgrades.
- **Coordinate Upgrades Before Long Breaks:** Before shutting down the game, dump all accumulated cash into ball speed and automated damage. Idle progress relies on consistent screen clears while you are away.
- **Use Milestones as a Build Guide:** If an achievement asks for a specific number of clicks, swap temporarily into click-damage upgrades. Once completed, pivot your resources back into automated ball systems.

By treating milestones as a step-by-step checklist rather than an afterthought, you eliminate the guesswork and build a balanced board capable of pushing past even the most resilient brick walls.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Idle Breakout Achievements

### Do idle breakout achievements reset when you prestige?
In most versions of the game, unlocked **idle breakout achievements** and their permanent stat bonuses remain tied to your save file and do not wipe when you prestige. Prestige resets your stage progression, active balls, and temporary cash, but your overall milestone records, trophies, and lifetime statistics are preserved.

### What is the fastest way to complete brick-count achievements?
The quickest method to complete brick-count tasks is to build a fleet of fully upgraded Scatter and Plasma Balls. Scatter Balls multiply the number of projectiles ricocheting across the screen, allowing you to register thousands of brick collisions per minute compared to slower, single-projectile options.

### Why did my progress toward a stage achievement stall?
If your stage progress stalls, your kinetic damage has likely fallen behind the exponential health scaling of the bricks. To break through the plateau, invest in Poison Balls to apply percentage-based damage over time, or trigger a prestige reset to gain permanent damage multipliers that will carry you into higher tiers.
