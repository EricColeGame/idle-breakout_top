### Mid Game (Levels 51–250)

Brick health jumps sharply here. Introduce the Plasma Ball to disintegrate clustered bricks simultaneously. Adding a Scatter Ball helps clean up peripheral stragglers while your heavier projectiles crack the primary wall formations.

### Late Game and Endless Scaling (Levels 251+)

Once you reach deep progression, determining your **idle breakout best ball** composition shifts entirely to Poison and Plasma synergy. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is objectively the idle breakout best ball for endless runs?
Community consensus and mathematical scaling point to the **Poison Ball** as the overall best ball for endless runs. Because it deals percentage-based damage over time that stacks with consecutive collisions, it never becomes obsolete against escalating brick health pools.

### Should I sell my Basic Balls once I unlock late-tier options?
Yes. As soon as your gold economy supports specialized units like the Plasma or Poison Ball, phase out Basic Balls. Basic Balls contribute negligible damage in later stages and consume projectile capacity better reserved for high-synergy balls.

### How does the Sniper Ball fit into a speed-clearing strategy?
The Sniper Ball serves as an executioner. While slow, it automatically tracks and targets bricks with the highest current health. Keeping one or two upgraded Sniper Balls on the field prevents ultra-dense gold or boss bricks from stalling your screen-clearing momentum.

### Why is movement speed prioritized over attack power on Poison Balls?
Poison Balls rely on applying and refreshing stacking status effects rather than flat impact damage. Higher movement speed leads to significantly more wall and brick collisions per second, multiplying poison application rates across the entire playing field.
