## Detailed Ball Breakdown: Stats, Mechanics, and Behavior

Examining each projectile individually reveals the hidden mathematical strengths that make specific compositions excel. When analyzing **idle breakout all balls**, calculating the return on investment (ROI) for speed versus power upgrades ensures you never waste precious gold.

### 1. The Basic Ball
The humble Basic Ball is your entry-level asset. While it lacks flashy visual effects or explosive chain reactions, its upgrade costs remain manageable during the opening levels. In early game phases, leveling Basic Ball speed offers a rapid stream of steady income, providing the financial runway needed to unlock specialized units.

### 2. The Plasma Ball
Plasma Balls are the undisputed kings of clearing densely packed brick clusters. Upon making contact with any brick, a Plasma Ball emits an energetic pulse that damages neighboring bricks within a set radius. 
* **Key Advantage**: Bypasses the need to strike every brick individually.
* **Best Paired With**: High speed upgrades to maximize frequency of radial bursts.

### 3. The Sniper Ball
Sniper Balls target high-health obstacles, moving methodically across the screen to hit high-durability blocks. Although their rate of fire is visually slower than other projectiles, their singular impact values dwarf those of lighter units. They eliminate gold bricks and armored barriers that would otherwise absorb hundreds of standard hits.

### 4. The Scatter Ball
The Scatter Ball introduces sheer mathematical chaos to the screen. Upon contacting a surface, it breaks into smaller child pellets that bounce erratically, hitting dozens of nearby blocks before dissipating. When fully upgraded, Scatter Balls trigger high hit counts, making them essential for triggering on-hit bonuses.

### 5. The Cannon Ball
Cannon Balls trade agility for brute kinetic force. Designed to crush high-tier bricks in fewer impacts, they excel when brick layouts become bulky and resilient. Investing in Cannon Ball power creates an efficient battering ram that softens up the board for faster units.

### 6. The Poison Ball
The Poison Ball is the backbone of deep-stage pushing. Instead of relying solely on instant kinetic damage, each strike leaves behind an acidic contagion. This damage-over-time (DoT) effect ticks continuously regardless of whether a ball is actively striking that brick. Across long sessions, poison ticks represent a massive portion of total cleared health pools.

                                    │

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which ball should I unlock first in Idle Breakout?
The Plasma Ball should always be your first priority purchase after starting the game. Its area-of-effect shockwave dramatically speeds up the destruction of clustered bricks, allowing you to earn cash significantly faster than relying on Basic Balls alone.

### Are Basic Balls worth upgrading in the late game?
No. In late-game stages, Basic Balls deal negligible damage relative to the multi-million health pools of advanced bricks. Keep their upgrades minimal and redirect your resources toward Poison, Plasma, and Scatter units.

### How does the Poison Ball work against high-health bricks?
The Poison Ball applies an infectious debuff on every brick it touches. This effect deals damage continuously over time regardless of whether other balls are striking that target. It is essential for late-game progression because it chips away massive health bars passively.

### What is the most effective setup for idle breakout all balls during prestige runs?
The most effective prestige composition combines 2 Poison Balls, 2 Plasma Balls, and 1 or 2 Scatter Balls. This setup covers every mechanical necessity: Poison delivers relentless background damage, Plasma clears dense clusters, and Scatter blankets the board to trigger on-hit mechanics.
